What happened

A good change shipped with a hidden side effect

Why it happens

A responder who slips can't climb back

A miss costs 0.12, a take earns 0.08, nothing fades. Fewer pings means fewer chances to recover, so four responders went quiet.

Five misses reach the floor; about seven takes climb back. Likely cause, still to confirm with engineering.
