What happened

A good change shipped with a hidden side effect

4.2 made two changes at once: it ranked nearby responders higher (a long-requested ask), and it cut the time to answer a ping from 90 to 60 seconds. The second one broke the safety net.

The "mostly seasonal, back in September" explanation does not hold: the break is on 12 Aug itself, and September data isn't in our snapshot (it ends 6–7 Sep).

Why it happens

A responder who slips can't climb back

The ranking gives +0.08 for a taken ping and takes off 0.12 for a miss or a turn-down, with no decay. Break-even is a 60% take rate. Below it, a score sinks to the floor, and a low score means fewer pings, which means fewer chances to earn points.

One bad run, then silence

missmissmissmissmissfloor

Five misses reach the floor. About seven takes are needed to get back to neutral, and the floor means almost no pings to take.

+++++++

Who got caught

Vesper, Meteor Mite, The Undertow, Farlight: pings fell from 75–86 to 11–17, with 53–64% missed. They now take 29–35%; the other twelve take 64–72%.

They weren't the worst performers before 4.2 (Vesper 82%, Meteor Mite 69%). The data fits "missed under 60s, then could not recover", not "targeted".

Confidence

Code and data agree strongly, but the cause is not yet proven: two changes shipped together, and Wen Li hasn't confirmed. Marcus's 14 Aug question about the weighting was never answered.

The worked example: The Undertow

Last ping taken 17 Aug. 14 pings since 12 Aug, 9 missed. Their handler filed 11 tickets, none answered.

The fix

Fix recovery and visibility together. Not a quiet number change.

1 · Recovery is automatic

Misses count less than turn-downs and fade over time. A one-off fresh start for the four.

2 · Responders are told

"You've missed a few recent pings, so we're offering you fewer for now. This eases off on its own." Asks nothing of them.

3 · Handlers can see why

Each responder's status is explained, and a fresh-start request is recorded in the existing audit log.

The 4.2 nearby-first ranking stays. Restoring the 90-second wait is proposed, pending Marcus and Wen Li, and isn't proven to be enough on its own.
